# Increment 3 report

Version: 0.3.0
Theme: Participant portal and evidence collection

## Delivered

The increment turns the recruitment journey into a functioning research workflow. After accepting consent, a participant can open a study portal, save an activity as a draft, submit it, upload supporting evidence and exchange messages with the research team. Researchers can inspect response status, communicate with participants, add internal notes, edit research structures, import participants and manage invitations.

## Automated verification

11 tests passed. Coverage includes authentication, project and study creation, participant enrolment, invitation acceptance, activity validation, observer-safe navigation, participant drafts and submissions, participant messaging, bulk import, edit interfaces and password-reset requests.
