5. Customer responsibilities

• Use the Platform only for lawful, authorised research purposes.

• Provide accurate participant notices and study information.

• Maintain appropriate user access controls and promptly remove access no longer required.

• Protect exports and downloaded Study Data.

• Do not configure studies to expose internal researcher AI outputs to participants.

• Do not instruct Politis Ltd to undertake unlawful automated decision-making or processing outside the agreed scope.

Data location. Citizen Centric is configured so that personal data is stored and processed in the United Kingdom and/or the European Union/European Economic Area. Politis Ltd does not intentionally transfer Study Data outside the UK/EU/EEA. If a restricted transfer becomes necessary, it must be documented and protected by an applicable UK GDPR transfer mechanism before the processing begins.
