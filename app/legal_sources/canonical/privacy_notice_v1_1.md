Citizen Centric Platform Privacy Notice

Platform, website and participant application

| Version | Effective date | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | 18 August 2026 | Politis Ltd |

This Privacy Notice explains how personal data is handled in connection with Citizen Centric, a research participation and study-management platform operated by Politis Ltd. It should be read alongside the study-specific privacy information and Participant Information Sheet supplied by the organisation running each study.

1. Who we are

Politis Ltd (company number 13661766) operates Citizen Centric. Registered office: The Old Courthouse, Orsett Road, Grays, Essex, England, RM17 5DD. ICO registration reference: ZB738312. Contact: info@politisconsulting.co.uk.

For Study Data collected for a customer study, the customer or research organisation normally acts as controller. Politis Ltd processes Study Data on the controller’s documented instructions and acts as processor. The study-specific notice identifies the controller for that study.

2. Personal data we handle

Citizen Centric may handle participant invitation/session information, consent and enrolment information, activity responses and drafts, participant messages, uploaded evidence and associated metadata, participant profile/contact information, local app state needed for secure operation, and technical/security records where generated by the service.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

The study controller is responsible for selecting and documenting the applicable UK GDPR Article 6 lawful basis and, where special-category data is processed, an applicable Article 9 condition. Citizen Centric requires governance details before a study is launched. Consent to participate in research is not necessarily the same as consent used as a UK GDPR lawful basis.

4. Special-category and sensitive information

Where a study involves special-category personal data, the study controller must provide appropriate transparency information, complete any required DPIA or ethics process, and configure the study accordingly. Politis Ltd processes it only within documented instructions and platform controls.

5. AI-assisted processing

The participant mobile application does not make direct calls to third-party AI services. Participant material and Study Data must not be used to train public or shared foundation models. Internal researcher AI outputs are not exposed through participant-facing interfaces.

6. Analytics, telemetry and similar technologies

The participant mobile application is not configured with advertising or marketing analytics SDKs. Operational telemetry, where enabled, is used for service reliability, diagnostics and security and is not used for advertising or marketing profiling.

7. Sharing and subprocessors

Politis Ltd uses service providers to host, secure and operate Citizen Centric under contractual terms appropriate to their role. The Subprocessor Schedule identifies the relevant planned/active Microsoft Azure services. Customer Study Data is not sold to third parties.

8. International transfers and data location

Citizen Centric is intended to hold and process customer and participant data in the United Kingdom and/or European Union/European Economic Area. Any restricted transfer requires an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism and safeguards where required.

9. Retention and deletion

Each study must have a controller-approved retention period or rule consistent with storage limitation, research requirements and applicable legal or ethical obligations. Participant local drafts and queued items are cleared on logout and after confirmed withdrawal/deletion controls where continued submission is no longer permitted.

Where a participant requests withdrawal plus deletion, identifiable participant Study Data held in active Citizen Centric systems will be deleted, subject only to a specific lawful retention requirement or applicable statutory/research exemption documented by the controller. Active-system deletion includes, where technically and lawfully capable of deletion, participant-linked research responses, drafts, messages, uploaded media/evidence, profile/account links and other participant-linked Study Data. A participant-selectable anonymisation option is not currently offered by the mobile application.

Information already irreversibly anonymised or aggregated so that the participant is no longer identifiable cannot be linked back to the participant and may continue to be used for the approved research or statistical purpose. Pseudonymised information remains personal data and is not treated as anonymous merely because an identifier has changed.

Deleted information may remain temporarily in protected backup copies until those backups expire under the applicable backup retention schedule. Backup copies are not used for ordinary research access. If a backup containing previously deleted data must be restored for genuine disaster recovery, deletion controls must be reapplied before restored data returns to normal use where technically feasible.

A minimum record may be retained where required for legal, security, accountability or regulatory reasons. It is minimised, access-restricted, retained only for the documented period, and must not be used to continue research participation after withdrawal.

10. Security

Politis Ltd applies role- and study-scoped access controls, participant-specific authorisation, encryption in transit, protected storage, audit/security logging, file validation and tenant-isolation controls. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Participant choices, withdrawal and deletion

Participants may stop participating in a study at any time using the study-specific withdrawal process. Once withdrawal takes effect, Citizen Centric stops permitting further participant activity or collection for that study and invalidates active participant access for the withdrawn study.

Withdrawal alone does not silently imply deletion. A participant can separately choose withdrawal plus deletion of identifiable active Study Data, or use the in-app account-deletion flow where available. The relevant study controller remains responsible for deciding and documenting any lawful exemption or required retention for Study Data. Politis Ltd implements deletion as processor in accordance with documented controller instructions and applicable law.

12. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and rights relating to automated decision-making. These rights are subject to statutory conditions and exemptions, including those that may apply to research. For Study Data, contact the controller named in the study-specific notice or Politis Ltd at info@politisconsulting.co.uk.

13. Complaints

You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how your personal data has been handled. We encourage you to contact the relevant study controller or Politis Ltd first so the issue can be investigated.

14. Changes to this notice

Material changes are versioned and published through the Citizen Centric legal centre. Version 1.0, effective 15 August 2026, remains preserved as a historical source. Study-specific notices are separately versioned so information presented at the time of consent can be retained historically.
