Citizen Centric Platform Privacy Notice

Platform, website and participant application

| Version | Effective date | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | 15 August 2026 | Politis Ltd |

This Privacy Notice explains how personal data is handled in connection with Citizen Centric, a research participation and study-management platform operated by Politis Ltd. It should be read alongside the study-specific privacy information and Participant Information Sheet supplied by the organisation running each study.

1. Who we are

Politis Ltd (company number 13661766) operates Citizen Centric. Registered office: The Old Courthouse, Orsett Road, Grays, Essex, England, RM17 5DD. ICO registration reference: ZB738312. Contact: info@politisconsulting.co.uk.

For personal data used to operate, secure, administer and support the Citizen Centric service, Politis Ltd may act as controller. For Study Data collected for a customer study, the customer or research organisation normally determines the purposes and means of the research and acts as controller; Politis Ltd processes that Study Data on the controller’s documented instructions and acts as processor. The study-specific notice identifies the controller for that study.

2. Personal data we handle

Account and access data, including researcher/organisation account details, participant invitation tokens, session and authentication information.

Study participation data, including consent status, enrolment and withdrawal status, activity responses, diary/reflection entries and structured responses.

Participant-submitted material, including text, photographs, documents and audio recordings, together with associated submission metadata.

Messaging and support data, including participant/researcher messages and support enquiries.

Technical and security data, including IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, diagnostic events, audit logs and security events where generated by the service.

Local app data needed for operation, such as drafts, cached participant-facing content and queued submissions awaiting upload.

AI-assisted processing inputs and outputs where an approved study enables an active AI feature, including transcription, translation, summaries, suggested codes, themes or related research-assistance outputs.

3. Purposes and lawful bases

Where Politis Ltd acts as controller, personal data is used to provide and secure the service, administer customer relationships, provide support, prevent misuse, maintain auditability, meet legal obligations and improve operational reliability. Depending on the activity, the lawful basis may be performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, or compliance with a legal obligation.

Where a customer acts as controller for Study Data, the customer is responsible for selecting and documenting the applicable UK GDPR Article 6 lawful basis and, where special-category data is processed, an applicable Article 9 condition. Citizen Centric requires those governance details before a study is launched. Consent to participate in research is not necessarily the same as consent used as a UK GDPR lawful basis.

4. Special-category and sensitive information

A study may involve special-category personal data where this is necessary for its approved research design. The study controller must identify the relevant Article 9 condition, provide appropriate transparency information, complete any required DPIA or ethics process, and configure the study accordingly. Politis Ltd processes such data only within the scope of documented instructions and platform controls.

5. AI-assisted processing

Citizen Centric may use server-side AI services for approved research-support functions such as transcription, translation, document processing, search, summarisation, suggested coding, thematic assistance and consistency or contradiction analysis. The participant mobile app does not make direct calls to third-party AI services. Internal researcher AI outputs are not exposed through participant-facing interfaces.

Participant material and Study Data must not be used to train public or shared foundation models. AI-assisted outputs are research-assistance tools and require appropriate human review; they must not be treated as automatically correct or used as the sole basis for decisions affecting participants. The study-specific notice must describe AI processing where required for transparency.

6. Analytics, telemetry and similar technologies

The participant mobile application is not configured with advertising or marketing analytics SDKs. Citizen Centric may use operational server-side telemetry, including Microsoft Application Insights, for service reliability, diagnostics and security where enabled and approved. This telemetry is not used for advertising, cross-site behavioural tracking or marketing profiling. Website cookies and similar technologies are described in the separate Cookie and Similar Technologies Policy.

7. Sharing and subprocessors

Politis Ltd uses service providers to host, secure and operate Citizen Centric. These providers act under contractual terms appropriate to their role. The current Subprocessor Schedule identifies the planned/active Microsoft Azure services used by the platform, including hosting/storage/database capabilities and Azure AI services. Customer Study Data is not sold to third parties.

8. International transfers and data location

Citizen Centric is intended to hold and process customer and participant data in the United Kingdom and/or European Union/European Economic Area. Where a supplier or support arrangement results in a restricted transfer outside the UK or EEA, the relevant controller and processor will use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism and supplementary safeguards where required. Customers should confirm the deployed Azure region and any applicable transfer safeguards as part of study onboarding and governance.

9. Retention

Citizen Centric does not apply an arbitrary universal retention period to Study Data. Each study must have an explicit controller-approved retention period or retention rule consistent with the UK GDPR storage-limitation principle, research requirements and any applicable legal or ethical obligations. Operational/security records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for their purpose and applicable legal obligations. Local drafts and queued items are removed or cleared in accordance with app workflows, including logout and withdrawal controls, subject to successful processing and any study-specific requirements.

10. Security

Politis Ltd applies technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including role- and study-scoped access controls, participant-specific authorisation, encryption in transit, protected storage, audit/security logging, malware/file validation controls, tenant isolation, and controls intended to prevent cross-participant and cross-organisation access. No internet-connected service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Participant choices, withdrawal and deletion

Participants can stop participating using the withdrawal process provided for their study. Withdrawal and deletion are separate: withdrawal does not automatically require deletion of information already lawfully processed or retained under the study’s approved governance. Participants may submit a deletion request where supported. The study controller determines the substantive response to data-rights requests relating to Study Data.

12. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and rights relating to automated decision-making. These rights are subject to statutory conditions and exemptions, including those that may apply to research. For Study Data, contact the controller named in the study-specific notice. You may also contact Politis Ltd at info@politisconsulting.co.uk.

13. Complaints

You may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you are unhappy with how your personal data has been handled. We encourage you to contact the relevant study controller or Politis Ltd first so the issue can be investigated.

14. Changes to this notice

Material changes will be versioned and published through the Citizen Centric legal centre. Study-specific notices are separately versioned so the information presented at the time of consent can be retained historically.
