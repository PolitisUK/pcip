Citizen Centric is designed with accessibility in mind, including support for screen-reader semantics, scalable text, clear focus order, accessible controls and non-colour-only status communication. Published accessibility claims should match functionality that has actually been tested and should not guarantee “full compatibility” with every assistive technology or device.

6. Security and data protection

Politis Ltd applies technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the platform, including HTTPS for data in transit, access controls, least-privilege principles, secure session handling and upload validation/scanning where deployed.

The participant mobile app should not transmit participant material directly to third-party AI endpoints. Any authorised AI processing is mediated through server-side services and governed by the relevant privacy and contractual documents.

Security or suspected data-protection incidents may be reported to info@politisconsulting.co.uk.
