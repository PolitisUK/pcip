Citizen Centric Accessibility Statement

| Version | Effective date | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | 15 August 2026 | Politis Ltd |

Politis Ltd wants Citizen Centric to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible. This statement covers the Citizen Centric public website, organisation/researcher portal and participant mobile application.

1. Our approach

We design and test Citizen Centric with accessibility in mind, including semantic labelling, readable text, text scaling, touch-target sizing, contrast, keyboard/focus behaviour where applicable, and simplified navigation. Accessibility is treated as an ongoing product requirement rather than a one-off certification exercise.

2. Current testing status

Automated and development-level accessibility checks have been completed across the current application and portal. Simulator/emulator testing has also been used during mobile QA. The release candidate has not yet completed the remaining physical-device VoiceOver and TalkBack checks, and some camera, microphone, permission-recovery and background/resume journeys require physical-device validation.

Accordingly, Politis Ltd does not claim that every part of Citizen Centric has been independently certified as fully conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA. The product is being developed toward accessible use and outstanding device-specific issues will be recorded and remediated through normal release controls.

3. Measures implemented

Accessible labels and semantics for interactive mobile controls.

Support for dynamic text sizing and layouts designed to tolerate larger text.

Contrast and touch-target review as part of UI quality checks.

Clear focus, headings and navigation structures in web interfaces where applicable.

Plain-language participant journeys and confirmation steps for significant actions.

Permission flows that explain why camera or microphone access is requested.

Accessibility checks included in regression and release QA.

4. Known limitations and outstanding validation

Full VoiceOver testing on a physical iPhone remains to be completed for the release candidate.

Full TalkBack testing on a physical Android device remains to be completed for the release candidate.

Physical-device validation remains for camera, microphone, photo-library/document-picker behaviour, OS permission denial/recovery and some background/offline media scenarios.

Third-party operating-system dialogs and device-specific behaviour may vary by platform version and hardware.

5. Alternative access and assistance

If you cannot access information or complete a task in Citizen Centric, contact us and describe the content or function you need, the device/browser you are using, and any assistive technology involved. We will consider a reasonable alternative and investigate the accessibility issue.

6. Feedback and contact

Accessibility enquiries and requests for assistance: info@politisconsulting.co.uk. Postal address: The Old Courthouse, Orsett Road, Grays, Essex, England, RM17 5DD.

7. Enforcement and legal context

Politis Ltd takes account of its obligations under applicable equality and accessibility law, including the Equality Act 2010. Customer organisations may have additional accessibility duties in relation to their own research services and content.

8. Review

This statement will be reviewed following completion of physical-device accessibility QA, after material platform changes, and periodically thereafter. Updates will be versioned in the Citizen Centric legal centre.
