# Citizen Centric Data Processing Agreement — Article 28 Schedule

UK GDPR Article 28 Schedule to the Citizen Centric Organisation SaaS Terms

| Version | Effective date | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | 18 August 2026 | Politis Ltd |

## 1. Roles and scope

For Study Data, the Customer acts as controller (or, where applicable, processor acting for another controller) and Politis Ltd acts as processor (or sub-processor). The Customer determines research purposes, lawful basis, study design, participant transparency information and retention requirements. Politis Ltd processes Study Data only to provide Citizen Centric and in accordance with documented instructions.

## 2. Assistance and deletion

Politis Ltd shall assist the Customer with documented erasure and data-subject requests using verified active-system deletion. This includes participant-scoped responses, drafts, messages, evidence/media records and the corresponding live storage objects where applicable. Access is revoked before any asynchronous deletion retry is marked complete.

## 3. Retention exceptions and backups

Where immediate deletion conflicts with a documented lawful retention requirement or preservation obligation, the Customer must document the specific exception, minimum category retained and retention period. Retained material must be restricted from ordinary research use where applicable. Backup copies expire through the normal secure backup lifecycle. If disaster recovery restores deleted Study Data, applicable deletion controls must be reapplied before normal processing resumes where technically feasible.

## 4. Audit and contact

Politis Ltd makes available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate Article 28 compliance, while protecting other customers’ confidentiality and security. Questions should be sent to info@politisconsulting.co.uk.
