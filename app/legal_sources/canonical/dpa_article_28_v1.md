Schedule A - Data Processing Agreement

UK GDPR Article 28 Schedule to the Citizen Centric Organisation SaaS Terms

| Version | Effective date | Operator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0 | 15 August 2026 | Politis Ltd |

This Data Processing Agreement (DPA) forms part of the Organisation SaaS Terms Agreement between the Customer identified in the applicable Order Form or agreement and Politis Ltd (the Service Provider). It applies where Politis Ltd processes personal data on behalf of the Customer in connection with Citizen Centric.

1. Roles and scope

For Study Data, the Customer acts as controller (or, where applicable, as a processor acting for another controller) and Politis Ltd acts as processor (or sub-processor, as applicable). The Customer determines the research purposes, lawful basis, study design, participant transparency information and retention requirements. Politis Ltd processes Study Data only to provide Citizen Centric and in accordance with documented instructions.

2. Processing details

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Provision, hosting, security, support and operation of Citizen Centric and the customer studies configured within it. |
| Duration | For the term of the customer agreement and thereafter only for the period necessary to return/delete data or comply with applicable law and documented retention requirements. |
| Nature/purpose | Collection, storage, organisation, retrieval, transmission, messaging, media handling, study administration, export, security monitoring, and approved AI-assisted research-support processing. |
| Data subjects | Research participants; prospective participants where invited; researchers; customer administrators; customer staff; and individuals whose personal data is contained in participant-submitted material. |
| Personal data | Identity/contact data where collected; invitation/session data; study responses; messages; photographs; documents; audio; consent and participation records; profile/preferences; technical/security metadata; support data; AI inputs/outputs where enabled. |
| Special-category data | May include health, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious/philosophical beliefs, trade-union membership, genetic/biometric data, sex life or sexual orientation, but only where the Customer has approved and documented the relevant study purpose, Article 9 condition and safeguards. |
| Frequency | Continuous or episodic for the duration of the relevant study and service relationship. |
| Retention | Study-specific, controller-approved retention. No universal 365-day default. Deletion/return at end of services subject to documented instructions, legal obligations and approved research retention. |

3. Documented instructions

Politis Ltd shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including with regard to transfers, unless required to do so by applicable law. If legally permitted, Politis Ltd will inform the Customer before processing required by law. The Agreement, Order Form, study configuration, governance records and written support instructions together constitute documented instructions.

If Politis Ltd considers an instruction to infringe UK GDPR or other applicable data-protection law, it will inform the Customer without undue delay and may suspend the affected processing pending clarification.

4. Confidentiality

Politis Ltd shall ensure that persons authorised to process Study Data are subject to an appropriate duty of confidentiality and receive access only as necessary for their role.

5. Security of processing

Taking account of the state of the art, implementation costs and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, Politis Ltd shall maintain measures appropriate to the risk. These include, as applicable:

role-based, organisation-scoped, study-scoped and participant-specific access controls;

authentication/session controls and separation of participant, researcher, organisation-admin and platform-admin privileges;

encryption in transit and protected cloud storage;

logging, monitoring and audit trails for security-relevant events;

file validation and malware-screening controls for uploaded material;

backup, resilience, recovery and secure deployment controls appropriate to the service;

tenant, study and object-level authorisation designed to prevent IDOR and cross-customer access;

secure development, dependency, secret and CI checks;

processes for incident response, vulnerability remediation and access revocation.

6. Sub-processors

The Customer gives general written authorisation for Politis Ltd to use the sub-processors listed in the current Subprocessor Schedule. Politis Ltd shall impose data-protection obligations on each sub-processor that provide protection equivalent in substance to the relevant requirements of this DPA. Politis Ltd remains responsible for the performance of its sub-processors to the extent required by applicable law.

Politis Ltd will provide reasonable advance notice of a material new sub-processor or replacement and allow the Customer to raise a reasonable data-protection objection. The parties will work in good faith to resolve the objection, including by considering an alternative where reasonably available.

7. Data-subject rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, Politis Ltd shall assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as reasonably possible, to respond to requests to exercise data-subject rights. Politis Ltd will not independently determine the merits of a request relating to Study Data unless required by law.

8. Personal-data breaches

Politis Ltd shall notify the Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal-data breach affecting Study Data and shall provide information reasonably available to assist the Customer with its obligations under Articles 33 and 34 UK GDPR. Notification by Politis Ltd does not constitute an admission of fault or liability.

9. DPIAs, prior consultation and governance assistance

Politis Ltd shall provide reasonable information and assistance relating to the platform’s processing to support the Customer’s DPIA, prior-consultation, ethics, lawful-basis, Article 9 and transfer assessments. The Customer remains responsible for decisions that depend on its research purpose, population, methodology and use of the results.

10. International transfers

Politis Ltd will configure the service so that Study Data is intended to be hosted and processed in the UK and/or EU/EEA. If processing involves a restricted transfer, Politis Ltd will ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism is in place where required, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, UK Addendum to approved EU Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy regulations, or another lawful mechanism, together with supplementary measures where appropriate.

11. AI services

Where approved AI Services process Study Data, they are subject to this DPA and the AI Services Schedule. Participant material must not be used to train public/shared foundation models. AI processing is server-side and internal researcher AI outputs must not be exposed to participants. Politis Ltd shall maintain human-oversight and provider-diligence controls proportionate to the feature and risk.

12. Return and deletion

At the Customer’s choice, on termination Politis Ltd shall return or delete Study Data after the applicable study/customer retention and exit process, unless applicable law requires continued storage. Where immediate deletion would conflict with an approved study retention requirement or lawful preservation obligation, the data will be restricted and deleted when that requirement ends. Backup deletion may follow the normal secure backup lifecycle.

13. Audit and information rights

Politis Ltd shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 obligations and allow for reasonable audits or inspections by the Customer or an independent auditor mandated by the Customer. Audits must protect other customers’ confidentiality and security, avoid unnecessary disruption, and ordinarily use available assurance evidence before intrusive testing. The Customer bears its own audit costs unless a material breach by Politis Ltd is established.

14. Order of precedence and liability

If this DPA conflicts with the Organisation SaaS Terms on the protection of personal data, this DPA prevails to the extent of the conflict. Liability is governed by the liability provisions of the Organisation SaaS Terms except where applicable law requires otherwise.
