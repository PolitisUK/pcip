11. How the Citizen Centric App Handles Your Information

The app connects to the configured Citizen Centric service using HTTPS. The production service address is configured by the service and is not entered by participants.

Participant session credentials are stored using the device platform's secure storage mechanisms and are cleared when the session is invalidated or the participant logs out.

Text drafts and queued submissions may be stored locally so that participant material is not lost if connectivity is interrupted. Queued text submissions use retry and duplicate-prevention controls.

Selected photographs, documents and voice recordings may remain on the device until upload is confirmed or the participant removes them. Media uploads may require manual retry where the backend does not support resumable transfer.

Local participant-specific caches and queued material are isolated by session and are cleared or made inaccessible when appropriate, including on logout and after withdrawal where continued submission is no longer permitted.

13. AI-Assisted and Automated Processing (Where Applicable)

The Citizen Centric backend may use approved automated tools to support research and platform operations. The study-specific version must describe only the uses that actually apply. Examples include:

Transcription: converting an audio recording into text using an approved server-side transcription service.

Document processing: extracting machine-readable text or structure from uploaded documents where this is part of the approved research workflow.

Research assistance: authorised server-side tools may support researchers with tasks such as organising or analysing research material. Internal researcher/AI outputs are not shown to participants unless a study expressly provides for this.

Security and file checks: automated malware, file-validation or other security controls may examine uploaded material to protect the service.

Participant material is submitted to Citizen Centric and is not sent directly from the mobile app to external AI services. Approved server-side processing is subject to access controls and researcher governance. Participant material will not be used to train public or shared foundation models. The study must not rely on a solely automated decision that produces legal or similarly significant effects on a participant unless a lawful, specifically approved process and all required safeguards are in place and clearly explained.

14. Confidentiality and Security

Access to identifiable study information is restricted according to role and study scope.

Data is protected in transit using HTTPS and by appropriate security controls in the service and cloud environment.

Participants should protect access to their own device. Where device-level storage is used, the app applies appropriate secure storage and session controls.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Suspected personal-data breaches are handled under the controller's and platform provider's incident-response procedures.

Citizen Centric platform processing note: Citizen Centric currently uses operational diagnostics, security logging and service-performance telemetry to maintain the reliability and security of the platform. This is not used for advertising, behavioural profiling or cross-site marketing. Google Analytics and marketing/advertising SDKs are not currently used in the participant mobile application. If any non-essential analytics technology requiring consent is introduced, it will be disabled by default until valid consent is obtained where PECR requires it. Current approved server-side AI service categories include Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Azure AI Speech, Azure AI Document Intelligence and Azure AI Search, used only where the relevant contracted/study feature is enabled. Upload security and malware scanning is also used for supported uploads. The participant mobile application does not send Participant Material directly to AI-provider endpoints. Internal researcher outputs such as suggested codes, themes, confidence scores, researcher notes and contradiction analysis are not participant-facing. Participant-facing transcription is not currently exposed as a mobile-app feature.

15. Retention, Deletion and Anonymisation

Identifiable study data: retained only for the controller-approved study retention period recorded before study launch, and no longer than necessary for the stated research purpose, subject to legal/regulatory requirements.

Research submissions / source media: retained for the controller-approved study retention period. Where Politis Ltd acts as processor, deletion/return follows the controller’s documented instruction and the applicable DPA.

Platform/security logs: retained only for as long as necessary for security, investigation, service integrity and legal accountability, under Politis Ltd’s operational retention schedule.

Local app copies: Drafts, queued submissions and selected media are retained only as needed to support the participant workflow and are cleared when no longer required, after confirmed upload where appropriate, on logout where applicable, or when the participant explicitly removes the item.

Once information has been irreversibly anonymised so that no individual is identifiable by reasonably available means, it is no longer personal data under UK GDPR and may be retained for research, statistical or analytical purposes in accordance with the approved research plan and applicable law. Pseudonymised information remains personal data and must continue to be protected.
