Responsible by design

Controls that support careful, trustworthy research.

Citizen Centric keeps participant access scoped to the relevant study and gives authorised teams the tools to manage consent, evidence and privacy requests.

Scoped access

Role-based researcher permissions and study-specific participant sessions.

Safer evidence

File validation, malware-screening states and protected downloads.

Privacy controls

Data export, deletion or anonymisation requests, and withdrawal workflows.

Accountability

Audit events and clear response states for important research actions.
