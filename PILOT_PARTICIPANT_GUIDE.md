# Pilot Participant Guide

This guide can be shared with residents and participants joining a pilot study.

## 1. Joining a study

1. Open the invitation link sent by the research team.
2. Review the study information.
3. Confirm consent and select Join study.
4. Keep your link safe for future activity access.

## 2. Completing activities

- Open each activity and follow the instructions.
- Save draft if you are not ready to submit.
- Submit when complete.
- Use the message area to contact the research team.

## 3. If something goes wrong

- Link not working: ask the research team to resend.
- Cannot upload a file: try a smaller supported format.
- Unsure what to answer: send a message and ask for clarification.

## 4. Privacy and trust

- Your responses are used for service improvement research.
- You may ask the team about your data at any point.
- If you wish to withdraw, contact the research team directly.

## 5. Accessibility support

If you need support with language, accessibility needs, or digital access, contact your council research team and request an assisted completion option.
